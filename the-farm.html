Lady B Willow Brook Farm

B

Lady B Willow Brook Farm has stood in the Hudson Valley since 1838. It is a working family farm and, more than that, the place our family returns to — for the quiet, the seasons, and the work of the land.

We grow heirloom tomatoes, apples, and peaches. We keep a flock of heritage hens, each named by a grandchild, who lay eggs in every shade from chocolate to sage. There are willows along the brook, a red barn, horses in the stable, and a long table by the fire where everyone gathers.

These pages are simply where we keep it all — four seasons of one family's corner of the valley.
